Beginnings...

Almost ten months ago, you were assigned to be part of a group of astronauts to board the Hope shuttle. Everything up to now has smoothly, but there's suddenly some interference...

"We're being pulled into that planet's center of gravity!" Your crewmate Anya was the first to say something after the terminal started sounding alarms. "What should we do, captain?" she looked to your brutish captain, Freya. Sweat dripped off of her furrowed brow. "If we don't try to push ourselves out of it now, we won't have a chance of escaping!" She pushed the thrusters as hard as they could go, but in the end the ship ran out of fuel.

Out of fuel now, your descent begins to pick up speed. You all hurry to gear up into whatever you can, and brace yourselves for impact.

BOOM!

Luckily, the impact wasn't as harsh as it would have been in decades past shuttles. You all file out of the shuttle and assess the exterior for damage. Visually, it was in pretty rough condition. However, only a dozen or so parts needed for takeoff were actually damaged. The main problem was the fuel.

"Wait!" Anya proclaimed. "I'm picking up readings for unrefined biofuel everywhere beneath the surface of this planet! If we can find a way to harvest and refine that, we can probably get our ship running enough to send out a distress signal." Then, Freya speaks up, "That's a good idea. In the mean time, I'll see what I can recover from the ship."
